Proportional Hazards Assumption Test

model1_resid <- cox.zph(model1)

model1_title <- fig_label("plot1", "Model 1 Assumption: Schoenfeld residuals vs time")

ggcoxzph(model1_resid, caption = model1_title)
  • Note: To interpret the graph, look to see if there is a time trend ** proportional hazard assumptions met if there is lack of a time trend ** also make sure to look at p-value. high-pvalue means assumption is met
